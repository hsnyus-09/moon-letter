# Community conduct

This project welcomes contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels. Be respectful, discuss ideas rather than attacking people, and make room for questions and constructive disagreement.

Harassment, discriminatory remarks, threats, sharing private information without permission, spam and deliberate disruption are not acceptable. Maintainers may remove content, close discussions or restrict participation when necessary to keep the community welcoming.

Report ordinary conduct concerns to the repository maintainers. For concerns containing private information, contact a maintainer privately through a contact method they explicitly provide; do not post private details in public issues. Security vulnerabilities follow SECURITY.md.

These expectations apply to project issues, reviews, discussions and other spaces managed by this project.
